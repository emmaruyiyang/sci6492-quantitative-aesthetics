Relief: TV and symmetry act on the height map, not the lit image.

    
Surface of revolution: with a texture, TV acts on the texture; blur and median too. Symmetry acts on the canvas.

    
Mesh texture: TV, blur and median act on the texture. Symmetry acts on the canvas.

    
3D gaussian splats: TV and symmetry act on the canvas view; blur and median don't apply.

    
Voxels: TV and symmetry act on the canvas view (the volume's own 3D smoothness is on the Field tab); blur and median don't apply.

    
Solids: TV and symmetry act on the canvas view; blur and median don't apply.
